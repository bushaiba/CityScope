03 / BEHIND THE DATA

Built for clarity.
Driven by data.

CityScope combines multiple public APIs to turn coordinates and JSON responses into readable comparisons. Weather and air quality are forecast estimates, not ground-station measurements.

01
City location search

Open-Meteo Geocoding API converts a search into coordinates.

02
Weather + air quality

Open-Meteo forecast and air-quality endpoints supply temperature, pollution indices and outlooks.

03
Daylight
